# AGENTS.md

## Workflow

- Always work from `tasks/spec.md` (what we are building) and `tasks/backlog.md` (what remains to do).
- Read both before starting any task.
- Keep both files up to date as development progresses:
  - Update `tasks/spec.md` when requirements, design, or behavior change.
  - Update `tasks/backlog.md` as tasks are added, started, completed, or re-prioritized.
- Do not consider a task done until the spec and backlog reflect it.
